# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community. Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, giving constructive feedback, respecting differing viewpoints and taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently exclude participants whose behavior violates these standards.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Security** tab. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information.
